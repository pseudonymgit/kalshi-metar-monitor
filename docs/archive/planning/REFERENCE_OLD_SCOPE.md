## Superseded assumptions

The following historical assumptions are superseded and must not be treated as current requirements:
- config-authoritative station lists
- threshold-based or window-heuristic alert triggering
- non-deterministic interpretation of transition paths
- observability behaviors that depend on live Kalshi endpoint calls
