## 2. Independent directional-edge re-derivation

Correct axis (`settlement_temp_f` vs `strike_price`), contemporaneous-only
(`fetch_date <= target_date`), correct units (°C→°F), 20 stations, 11,047 labelable days.

### 2a. Aggregate confusion matrix (worker's `lead<=1` preference — mixed lead)

| | TP | FP | TN | FN |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | 198 | 507 | 9805 | 537 |

- Base cross rate: **0.0665** (6.65% of days cross the strike)
- Precision (cross): **0.281** (4.22× the base rate)
- Accuracy: 0.906 vs majority-baseline 0.934 (**accuracy is BELOW baseline by 2.8%**)
- MCC: **+0.224**
- Net EV (cross bet): **+0.19** (matches worker's upper bound)

The forecast is a **biased cross-predictor**: it calls "cross" 40.5% of the time when
only 6.65% actually cross (507 false cross vs 198 true cross). The positive EV is real
(the precision lift 0.28/0.067 survives the 2.05¢ round-trip + 0.43¢ taker), but it
comes from a low-win-rate (28%), high-volume bet, and the headline is inflated by the
lead and station artifacts below.

### 2b. Lead decomposition — the critical finding

The worker's `nwp_forecast(lead=1)` prefers lead ≤ 1, i.e. it **silently uses lead=0
(same-day fetch) when available**, falling back to lead=1, then to much longer leads.
Actual lead distribution of predictions:

| Lead used | n predictions |
|---|---|
| lead=0 (same-day) | 5654 |
| lead=1 (true h-24) | 4434 |
| lead=68–91 (climatology fallback) | ~950 |

Re-derivation with **exact lead**:

| Lead | n | base | precision | net EV | TP | FP |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| lead=0 (same-day) | 10088 | 0.0687 | 0.276 | **+0.182** | 190 | 499 |
| lead=1 (true h-24) | 4434 | 0.0386 | 0.213 | **+0.152** | 32 | 118 |

**The "+0.15 to +0.19 h-24 edge" is actually a lead=0 (same-day) edge.** True h-24
(lead=1) is +0.15 EV but rests on only 32 correct cross-calls over 4434 days (0.7%
cross-hit rate) at a 3.9% base — a knife-edge estimate. The worker's label "h-24" is
a **contemporaneity error** (same-day forecast ≠ 24-hour-ahead forecast).

- The top-4 EV stations (KATL, KSAT, KBOS, KDCA) are **all short-history (2026-only,

### 2d. Temporal stability

Quarterly net EV is **unstable**:
- 2025: mostly positive (+0.05 → +0.57, with a +0.57 outlier in Oct 2025)
- **2026-01: −0.007; 2026-02: +0.027** (near-zero/negative — the deep-history stations
  dominate here, before short-history stations flood in)
- 2026-03 → 2026-09: positive but inflated by the short-history station influx

The 2026-01/02 dip is consistent with the deep-history stations' true edge being
marginal-to-zero in that window, and the "strong" 2026 edge being partly a
short-sample artifact.

## 3. Verdict

**Is the h-24 NWP directional edge real, robust, well-distributed, fee-surviving — or
another artifact?**

It is a **partially-real but mislabeled and fragile edge**, not the clean finding the
worker reported:

1. **Real (not noise):** the NWP forecast has genuine magnitude skill (corr 0.79 with
   settlement) and genuine directional skill (MCC +0.22). The °C→°F fix was necessary
   and the prior "no edge" verdict was indeed a unit artifact. This part of the worker's
   claim is CORRECT.

2. **Mislabeled (contemporaneity error):** the "+0.15–0.19 h-24 edge" is dominated by
   **lead=0 (same-day) forecasts**, not true h-24 (lead=1). The worker's `lead<=1`
   preference silently used same-day data. True h-24 alone is +0.15 EV on 32 correct
   calls over 4434 days — a knife-edge, not a robust estimate.

3. **Fragile (station concentration):** the headline +0.19 is inflated by 4 short-history
   stations (KATL/KSAT/KBOS/KDCA) contributing +0.30→+0.78 from 5–13 predictions each,
   plus 2 negative stations (KLAS/KSEA). This is the same station-heterogeneity
   concentration artifact flagged in earlier QC.

4. **Fee-surviving:** yes, the precision lift (0.21–0.28 vs 0.04–0.07 base) clears the
   2.05¢ round-trip + taker fee — but only because the bet is a rare-event (3.9–6.9% base)
   high-precision bet, not a robust, high-confidence edge.

**Bottom line for the pipeline:** the °C→°F unit fix is real and should be propagated
everywhere. But do NOT promote "h-24 NWP directional edge" as a tradeable signal on the
strength of commit 708a3ea25. The defensible core is (a) a **same-day** NWP forecast edge
(+0.18 EV, robust across deep-history stations), and (b) a **true h-24** edge of +0.15 EV
that is real but thin and needs (i) correct lead labeling, (ii) station-heterogeneity
controls, and (iii) a cross-validation / temporal-holdout test before it is trusted.
