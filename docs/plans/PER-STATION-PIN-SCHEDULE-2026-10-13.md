### Method (read this — it matters)

Two Brier columns, because they answer different questions:

- **brier_reanchor** — the literal study rule: `T = floor(hsf@pin)+1`, p = the
  study's rate_1h window-max scan (13:00–16:45), strict label, canonical truth.
  Under re-anchoring the strike RISES with the pin, so this curve is
  **anchor-dominated** (always favours the earliest, easiest strike). It is NOT
  evidence about signal timing and is shown only for completeness.
- **brier_fixedT** — signal timing, isolated: hold the target at the canonical
  13:30 strike (`T = floor(hsf@13:30)+1`) and score the rate_1h nowcast BOUNDED
  to `13:00..pin` (no look-ahead). This curve wants the pin **LATE** (more of the
  day observed).
- **spread (near)** — median near-money (mid ∈ [0.2,0.8]) bid-ask at that
  station-local pin; the fill wants the pin **EARLY** (overnight spreads are
  narrower).

**Recommendation rule:** minimise `spread_z + brier_fixedT_z` — the crossing of the
signal-timing and fill-cost curves.

## Findings

- **The signal wants the pin late, monotonically, at every station.** brier_fixedT falls from 13:00 to 15:00 for all 20 stations (e.g. KATL 0.567 → 0.102, KSAT 0.638 → 0.073): the nowcast is cold at 13:00 and sharpens as the day's max is observed.
- **The spread does NOT differentiate inside 12:00–15:00 local.** Near-money (mid ∈ [0.2,0.8]) median spread is ~1 tick (0.01) across the whole window at essentially every station; the wide-spread regimeidentified earlier sits in the 17–20Z (afternoon) window, outside this sweep. Sample counts are thin (n=4–7 station-days per pin), so the spread column is weakly identified.
- **Consequence: the mechanical pick is the late edge (15:00 local, 9h lead) at all 20 stations** — the signal-timing term dominates because the fill-cost term is flat. This is *not* a per-station-differentiated schedule; the data does not support differentiation within 12:00–15:00.
- **The binding constraint is not in this sweep:** going later than 15:00 keeps improving the signal but (a) the wide-spread afternoon window opens ~17–20Z and (b) settlement/close bounds the lead. The honest read is that the recommend-late result is bounded by those, and a 13:30→15:00 move is the defensible in-window delta, not a station map.
