# Weather Engine — Processing-Edge Diagnostic Roadmap

**Date:** 2026-08-22  
**Status:** Read-only diagnostic plan; no sweep, code, configuration, scheduler, source, or trading-path changes performed.  
**Hypothesis:** Predictive information may exist in the source data but be destroyed or hidden by label construction, time/station joins, duplicate reduction, calibration/fusion, gates, market eligibility, execution assumptions, settlement mapping, sizing, or concentration.

## Receipt / current evidence

The current evidence does **not** justify killing the project or claiming an edge:

- The 2026-08-19 gap audit records the software suite as strong QA but incomplete trading validation. The bounded research sweep was **45.95% holdout accuracy vs 47.34% persistence**, **-$7.48 proxy P&L**, and **-1.599 proxy Sharpe**.
- The 2026-07-31 GEFS/Kalshi report records approximately **88% directional accuracy but negative P&L** under real fees/prices; this is a materially different pipeline/target and must be reconciled, not averaged with the 45.95% result.
- Goldilocks' **3.92% precision** (2026-08-03) is contaminated by 699 absurd `awc_tgftp` temperatures; the two apparent true positives were artifacts. UTC/local-day grouping, UTC-hardcoded late-day gating, plateau re-crossing, and rounded labels are additional loss paths.
- The cascade zero-trade diagnosis attributes attrition to member correlation **rho=0.657**, effective sample size about **1.50**, and an edge below the **0.02** gate even at unanimity.
- The full fire-rate audit finds only 15 of 40 signals firing at >=1%; several signals are zero-fire because required data/interfaces are absent, while `persistence`, `simple_trend`, and `metar_trend` are 100% fire and redundant. The correlation report recommends removing pairs with `|rho| >= 0.7` and identifies exact redundancy at rho=1.0.
- Trajectory and frontal/trajectory-related tests are not safe to promote: the signal sweep reports confirmed trajectory at 51.4% versus neutral at 62.5%, and says not to wire trajectory/frontal gates after >10pp drops.

These are diagnostic clues, not a project-level kill decision. A weak item receives `FAIL`, `INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE`, `BLOCKED`, or `PARK`; the project remains alive while independent items are audited.

## End-to-end audit receipt / roadmap

### P0 — Truth and accounting (read-only first)

1. Freeze an input manifest and hashes for settlement receipts, contract rules, source/vintage tables, station map, market snapshots, code/config, and environment.
2. Build the event-level reconciliation ledger: exact identity, local-day/timezone, decision cutoff, source availability, revisions, duplicates, overlap, station mapping, HIGH/LOW, strike/bucket, and final receipt hash.
3. Run deterministic fixture tests for labels, boundaries, timestamps/DST, station joins, duplicate/revision behavior, label isolation, and missing/unknown mappings. Run twice and require byte-identical canonical output.
4. Emit a stagewise attrition table from eligible event to signal to gate to eligible market to fill; include every rejection and zero-fire/one-direction row.

**P0 stop conditions:** unresolved rule/source or mapping, target-day access, absent availability, duplicate overwrite, unexplained join loss, non-exhaustive buckets, receipt/hash mismatch, nondeterministic output, or missing denominator. These are `BLOCKED`, not negative edge.

### P1 — Forecast and calibration diagnosis

1. Evaluate each lane/signal independently per station × HIGH/LOW before fusion; compare persistence, climatology, modal, always-direction, and simple-trend baselines.
2. Separate raw signal output, calibrated probability, fused output, and gate output. Report accuracy, exact bucket Brier/log loss/ECE, MAE, confidence intervals, effective sample size, and block-bootstrap/serial-correlation notes where supported.
3. Recompute correlation using full canonical history and remove only demonstrably redundant clones; do not use aggregate fusion to conceal a weak component.
4. Keep the untouched holdout sealed; use nested selection and prior-fold-only calibration. Do not run another broad tuning sweep.

**P1 disposition:** `PASS` only means the item passes its own contract with adequate evidence; weak or underpowered items are `PARK`/`INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE`. No individual failure kills unrelated lanes or the project.

### P2 — Execution and risk (only after P1 screen-positive evidence)

1. Replay real or conservative historical quotes/order books with exact fees, ask/bid fills, partial/no-fill, latency, slippage, adverse selection, and markouts.
2. Recompute strike-based settlement and P&L, then canonical sizing/drawdown and concentration with full rejection receipts.
3. Report the smallest station/market subset whose Track A/B edge survives Track C costs; do not promote from midpoint/proxy prices.

**Promotion boundary:** no production, paper-trading, or live-capital promotion until the validation matrix and Track A/B/C receipts pass, with independent validation sign-off. The 500 OOS / Sharpe >0.3 / three-stage walk-forward figures remain the existing pre-wiring contract in `FP-MULTI-SIGNAL-FUSION.md`; they are not to be relaxed or silently substituted by aggregate directional accuracy.

## Final disposition

- **Project:** KEEP ALIVE / continue diagnostic validation.
- **Current global edge claim:** `UNKNOWN/OVERSTATED`, not a kill.
- **Most urgent likely processing losses:** truth/target mismatch; UTC/local and vintage alignment; station/event identity; corrupted METAR values; duplicate/revision handling; opaque gate attrition; proxy execution prices; direction-versus-strike settlement.
- **Current lane disposition:** no lane is promoted by this receipt. Lanes with known contamination or harmful gate evidence are `PARK` pending repair and causal replay; zero-fire/data-absent lanes are `NOT_APPLICABLE` or `BLOCKED`, not `FAIL`; independent screen-positive subsets may advance without requiring every item to succeed.
